How Can We Help?

Find answers to common questions about Interview Management.

🔐 How do I login?

Use your registered username and password on the login page. Candidates and interviewers will automatically be taken to their respective dashboards.

📝 How do I register?

Candidates and interviewers can register using the registration options available on the home page.

📅 How is an interview scheduled?

HR views the candidate, selects an interviewer and schedules the interview with the appropriate date and time.

📍 How can I track my interview?

Candidates can login to their dashboard to view their scheduled and completed interviews.

⭐ Where can I see my result?

After the interviewer submits feedback, the candidate can view the available feedback from their dashboard.

🔑 Forgot my password?

Use the Forgot Password option on the login page to receive a password reset link by email.
