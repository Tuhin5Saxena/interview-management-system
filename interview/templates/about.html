About Interview Management

Simplifying the interview process for candidates, interviewers and HR.

Who We Are

Interview Management is a platform designed to make the interview process simple, organized and efficient. It connects candidates, interviewers and HR through one centralized system.

What We Provide

📅

Interview Scheduling

Schedule interviews and assign the appropriate interviewer to each candidate.

👤

Candidate Management

Candidates can create accounts and track their interview progress.

👨‍💼

Interviewer Management

Interviewers can view their assigned interviews and manage the interview process.

📍

Interview Tracking

Keep track of scheduled, ongoing and completed interviews.

📝

Feedback & Evaluation

Interviewers can submit feedback and evaluation after completing an interview.

📊

HR Management

HR can manage candidates, interviewers and monitor the overall interview process.

Our Goal

Our goal is to create a centralized interview management system that reduces manual work, improves communication and makes the interview process easier for everyone involved.
