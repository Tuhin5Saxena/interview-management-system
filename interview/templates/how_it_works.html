How Interview Management Works

A simple and organized interview process for everyone.

👨‍🎓 For Candidates

1

Register

Create your candidate account.

2

Login

Access your candidate dashboard.

3

Track

View your scheduled interviews.

4

Interview

Attend your scheduled interview.

5

Result

Check your feedback and result.

👨‍💼 For Interviewers

1

Register

Create your interviewer account.

2

Login

Access your interviewer dashboard.

3

View

See interviews assigned to you.

4

Interview

Conduct the candidate interview.

5

Feedback

Submit candidate feedback.

👨‍💼 For HR

1

Login

HR logs into the HR dashboard to manage the interview process.

2

View Candidates

HR can view registered candidates and their information.

3

Assign Interviewer

HR selects an appropriate interviewer for the candidate.

4

Schedule Interview

HR schedules the interview by selecting the date, time, candidate and interviewer.

5

Monitor Process

HR can monitor interviews, view feedback and track the overall interview process.
